Your users don’t care that your AI project is model agnostic. In my last project, I spent countless hours ensuring that the LLMs running my services could be swapped out as easily as possible. I couldn&rsquo;t touch a device with an internet connection without hearing about the latest benchmark-breaking model and it felt like a clear priority to ensure I could hot swap models with minimal collateral damage.
