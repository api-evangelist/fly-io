Recently, I suggested that The Future Isn’t Model Agnostic , that it’s better to pick one model that works for your project and build around it, rather than engineering for model flexibility. If you buy that, you also have to acknowledge how important comprehensive model evaluation becomes. Benchmarks tell us almost nothing about how a model will actually behave in the wild, especially with long contexts, or when trusted to deliver the tone and feel that defines the UX we’re shooting for.
